Adversarial training is a machine learning technique that improves a model's robustness by intentionally exposing it to carefully crafted, misleading inputs during the training process. These adversarial examples are designed to look nearly identical to normal data but contain subtle perturbations that would typically cause the model to make incorrect predictions. By including these challenging cases in training and teaching the model to correctly classify them alongside regular examples, the system learns to recognize and resist such manipulations. This approach functions like a vaccination for AI models: just as vaccines expose the immune system to weakened threats to build resistance, adversarial training exposes models to controlled attacks to strengthen their defenses against real-world manipulation attempts, resulting in systems that are more reliable and secure when deployed in practical applications where malicious actors might try to exploit vulnerabilities.
